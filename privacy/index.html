Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how XI Hunt processes information when you use the XI Hunt mobile game and related support pages.

1. Who is responsible

XI Hunt is operated by the developer identified in the applicable App Store or Google Play listing. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to support.virasea@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: email address, Supabase authentication identifier and authentication status. Passwords are processed by Supabase Auth and are not visible to XI Hunt.
  • Profile and social information: display name, friend tag/code, friend requests, friendships and user reports.
  • Gameplay information: submitted answers, matches, moves, scores, rankings, achievements, rewards, inventory, game settings and anti-abuse records.
  • Technical information: a pseudonymous guest/device identifier, session and connection status, app/platform information, IP address and operational logs processed by our service providers.
  • Advertising information: with the choices and permissions available on your device, Google Mobile Ads may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device/app information, ad interactions and diagnostics to provide, limit, measure and protect ads.
  • Support information: your email address and any details you choose to include when contacting support.

XI Hunt does not currently use a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. These GitHub Pages do not use cookies, analytics or tracking forms.

3. Why we process information

We process information to create and secure accounts, operate solo and multiplayer gameplay, validate answers, maintain rankings and rewards, prevent fraud and abuse, provide support, show ads under the consent choices available to you, comply with law and improve service reliability.

Where applicable, our legal bases are performance of the service you request, legitimate interests in operating and protecting XI Hunt, consent for advertising where required, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Service providers and disclosures

We use Supabase for authentication, database, realtime and server functions; Google AdMob for advertising; and Apple App Store and Google Play for distribution and platform services. These providers may process information under their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service.

We do not sell personal information. XI Hunt does not allow other players to see your email address.

5. International processing

Our providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, transfers are protected using the legal safeguards offered by the relevant provider and applicable law.

6. Retention and account deletion

Account and gameplay information is kept while your account is active and as needed to operate and secure XI Hunt. When you delete your account, the login and authentication account are deleted, direct profile identifiers are removed or anonymized, and active friendships are removed. Historical match, ranking-integrity, moderation and transaction records may be retained in anonymized or pseudonymized form so that other users' records, fraud prevention and legal obligations remain intact.

Operational logs and advertising data are retained according to the provider's settings and policies. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and meet legal obligations.

See Account Deletion for in-app and web-request instructions.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, receive a copy of your data, withdraw consent and complain to a data-protection authority. You can change system advertising permissions in device settings. Where required, XI Hunt also presents Google's advertising privacy choices.

To exercise a right, email support.virasea@gmail.com. We may need to verify that you control the relevant account.

8. Children

XI Hunt is not directed to children below the minimum age required to consent to an online service in their country. If you believe a child provided personal information without valid permission, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

9. Security

We use access controls, authenticated server functions, row-level database policies and other reasonable safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as XI Hunt or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.

11. Contact

support.virasea@gmail.com
